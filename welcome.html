You have a battery now

Using a tracked site drains it. Being away refills it. The toolbar badge and popup show the charge, and a warning appears when it runs low.

Empty means blocked

At zero the tracked sites go dark for 10 minutes while the charge climbs back. Getting in early takes a question and a line on why, fresh every time.
